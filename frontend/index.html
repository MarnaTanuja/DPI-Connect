DPI Connect

Overview

How a connection moves from request to a shared dataset:

  1. Upload a dataset. We infer its field structure automatically — no manual schema-writing.
  2. Request a connection to another organization, pointing at what you have and what shape of data you want from them.
  3. We score the fit immediately — how well their fields map onto yours — and show you the breakdown.
  4. They review and consent. They see the same similarity report before deciding, and sign a short agreement.
  5. The channel opens over a signed secure exchange, and you pull one combined dataset — your records plus theirs, mapped into your shape.
